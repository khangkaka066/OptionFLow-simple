MUC DICH CUA TUNG BIEU DO
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) GEX (Gamma Exposure) by Strike
   - Do dealer se phai hedge bao nhieu khi gia di chuyen qua strike do.
   - Net GEX duong -> dealer LONG gamma -> dealer mua khi gia giam, ban khi
     gia tang -> HAM gia lai, thi truong co xu huong "range-bound".
   - Net GEX am -> dealer SHORT gamma -> dealer ban khi gia giam, mua khi gia
     tang -> KHUECH DAI bien dong, thi truong de "trend/chay mach".
   - Cac muc quan trong tren chart:
     * Gamma Wall / Call Resistance: strike co GEX duong lon nhat phia tren
       spot -> vung kha nang chan gia tang (resistance).
     * Put Support: strike co GEX am lon nhat phia duoi spot -> vung kha nang
       chan gia giam (support).
     * Gamma Flip: strike ma Net GEX doi dau (tu am sang duong theo strike
       tang dan) -> ranh gioi giua che do "kim ham" va "khuech dai".

2) DEX (Delta Exposure) by Strike
   - Do dealer dang net long/short bao nhieu delta (huong) tai moi strike.
   - Net DEX am lon -> dealer dang short delta manh -> ap luc mua vao de
     hedge khi gia tang (co the ho tro dop tang).
   - Net DEX duong lon -> dealer dang long delta -> ap luc ban ra khi gia
     tang (co the can tro dop tang, tao resistance).
   - Delta Flip: strike ma Net DEX doi dau -> vung chuyen tiep ve dong luon
     hedge cua dealer.
   - DEX phan anh "ap luc mua/ban co dinh huong" ro hon GEX (GEX doi xung ca
     hai chieu, DEX co dau ro rang theo huong).

3) OI by Strike (thanh Call/Put xep canh nhau, khong nhan IV)
   - Cho biet KHOI LUONG hop dong dang mo tai moi strike, khong quan tam
     dinh gia (IV) hay do nhay (gamma/delta).
   - Dung de xac dinh "vung tap trung thanh khoan" (nhieu OI) - thuong trung
     voi cac muc GEX/DEX lon vi OI la thanh phan cau thanh GEX/DEX.
   - Strike co Call OI vuot troi -> nhieu khoan the hien ky vong/hedge tang
     gia hoac ban call de thu premium tai do.
   - Strike co Put OI vuot troi -> nhieu khoan the hien ky vong/hedge giam
     gia hoac ban put de thu premium tai do.
   - OI by Strike la "bang chung tho" (raw), chua qua trong so gamma/delta,
     nen dung de XAC NHAN lai muc GEX/DEX quan trong co that su co volume
     lon dung sau hay khong, tranh bi nhieu boi 1-2 hop dong outlier co
     gamma/delta cao bat thuong.

4) OI x IV by Strike (2 thanh Call/Put + duong IV tren truc phu)
   - Giong OI by Strike nhung them lop DINH GIA QUYEN CHON (Implied
     Volatility) tai moi strike.
   - IV cao tai 1 strike -> thi truong dinh gia rui ro/bien dong lon hon tai
     vung gia do (thuong thay IV skew tang manh o cac strike xa ATM, dac
     biet la put OTM - "volatility skew").
   - IV thap bat thuong tai vung nhieu OI -> thi truong tin rang gia se "bi
     hut" ve do va it bien dong khi cham strike (phu hop voi vung GEX duong
     lon, dealer long gamma se ham gia).
   - IV cao bat thuong tai vung nhieu OI -> canh bao kha nang breakout/move
     manh neu gia cham vao strike do (khop voi vung GEX am, dealer short
     gamma se khuech dai bien dong).
   - Dung OI x IV de tra loi cau hoi: "Muc OI lon nay la muc THAT SU se ham/
     day gia, hay chi la noise dinh gia thap khong dang tin cay?"

QUY TRINH DOC KET HOP (THEO THU TU) DE RA TRADE PLAN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

BUOC 1 - Xac dinh CHE DO THI TRUONG (Net GEX)
   - Mo tab GEX, nhin Net GEX o dau dashboard.
   - Net GEX duong -> uu tien chien luoc MEAN-REVERSION / FADE bien dong
     (ban premium, danh range, cho gia bat lai tu Call Resistance / Put
     Support).
   - Net GEX am -> uu tien chien luoc TREND-FOLLOWING / BREAKOUT (mua
     momentum khi gia pha Gamma Flip, tranh fade som).

BUOC 2 - Khoanh vung RANGE giao dich trong ngay (GEX)
   - Bien tren: Call Resistance / Gamma Wall duong gan spot nhat.
   - Bien duoi: Put Support gan spot nhat.
   - Diem trung tam/ranh gioi che do: Gamma Flip.
   - Neu spot dang o giua Put Support va Call Resistance va Net GEX duong
     -> uu tien range-trade trong khung do.
   - Neu spot da vuot qua Gamma Flip theo huong nao -> canh bao thi truong
     co the chuyen sang che do khuech dai bien dong theo huong do.

BUOC 3 - Kiem tra AP LUC HUONG (DEX)
   - Nhin Net DEX va Delta Flip.
   - Net DEX am manh + spot gan/duoi Put Support -> co the co ho tro mua
     hedge cua dealer, tang xac suat bat lai tai Put Support (tin hieu ung ho
     LONG tai support).
   - Net DEX duong manh tai vung tren Call Resistance -> ap luc ban hedge
     cua dealer co the can gia tai do (tin hieu ung ho SHORT/chot loi tai
     resistance).
   - Neu DEX va GEX cung chieu (vd Call Resistance trung voi vung DEX duong
     lon) -> muc do TIN CAY resistance/support cao hon vi ca 2 loai hedge
     deu tao ap luc cung huong.

BUOC 4 - XAC NHAN thanh khoan that su bang OI by Strike
   - Doi chieu cac muc Gamma Wall / Call Resistance / Put Support / Delta
     Flip vua tim o Buoc 2-3 voi cot OI tai strike do.
   - Muc GEX/DEX lon nhung OI (Call+Put) tai do lai nho -> do tin cay THAP,
     de bi "gay" khi co lenh lon moi vao, khong nen dat entry/stop qua sat.
   - Muc GEX/DEX lon VA OI cung lon (Call OI hoac Put OI vuot troi ro ret)
     -> do tin cay CAO, day la vung "tuong" that su, phu hop dat entry/stop/
     target quanh do.

BUOC 5 - Danh gia RUI RO BIEN DONG bang OI x IV by Strike
   - Nhin duong IV tai chinh cac strike vua xac nhan o Buoc 4.
   - IV thap tai vung OI lon, dac biet la vung GEX duong (long gamma) ->
     kich ban "ham gia" duoc cung co them boi dinh gia thap -> uu tien SELL
     PREMIUM (short strangle/iron condor quanh Call Resistance - Put
     Support) neu dang o che do range-bound.
   - IV cao tai vung OI lon gan Gamma Flip hoac vung GEX am -> kich ban
     "breakout that su neu gia cham toi" duoc cung co boi dinh gia cao ->
     TRANH ban premium tai do; neu muon tham gia, uu tien MUA (long call/
     put hoac debit spread) theo huong breakout, KHONG fade.
   - So sanh IV giua 2 canh (call side vs put side): ben nao IV cao hon (vi
     du put IV cao hon call IV - "put skew") cho biet thi truong dang so
     giam gia hon la tang gia -> co the nghieng size/bias ve phia phong thu
     downside (mua put hedge re hon tuong doi, hoac ban call premium re hon
     tuong doi).

GHEP THANH 1 TRADE PLAN HOAN CHINH (VI DU KHUNG MAU)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Che do thi truong: Net GEX [duong/am] -> [range-trade / trend-follow].
2. Bien range: Put Support = X, Call Resistance = Y, Gamma Flip = Z.
3. Xac nhan bang OI: Call OI tai Y la [cao/thap], Put OI tai X la [cao/
   thap] -> do tin cay [cao/thap].
4. Xac nhan bang DEX: Delta Flip = W, huong ap luc hedge tai X/Y la
   [mua/ban].
5. Xac nhan bang IV: IV tai X = a%, tai Y = b% -> [ham gia/canh bao breakout]
   tai tung bien.
6. QUYET DINH:
   - Neu ca 4 lop (GEX, DEX, OI, IV) DONG THUAN -> vao lenh voi size binh
     thuong/full, dat stop ngay ngoai muc da xac nhan (vi du duoi Put
     Support mot chut cho lenh long).
   - Neu chi 2-3 lop dong thuan -> giam size, hoac cho gia phan ung ro rang
     tai muc (nen/wick tu choi) truoc khi vao thay vi vao truoc.
   - Neu cac lop MAU THUAN (vi du GEX bao ham nhung IV lai cao bat thuong
     tai do, hoac OI qua mong) -> DUNG NGOAI, cho tin hieu ro hon, KHONG
     ep lenh.
7. Quan ly lenh trong ngay:
   - Gia cham Put Support/Call Resistance co OI+DEX xac nhan -> uu tien
     canh mua/ban tai do theo huong fade (neu con trong che do Net GEX
     duong).
   - Gia pha Gamma Flip voi khoi luong/toc do bat thuong -> chuyen sang tam
     the trend-follow, HUY cac lenh fade dang cho phia con lai, chi con
     canh mua theo huong breakout, uu tien theo cac strike co IV cao vua
     xac nhan o Buoc 5 (thi truong da dinh gia truoc kha nang nay).

LUU Y
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Day la mo hinh xap xi ca nhan (Black-Scholes tren OI cong khai), khong
  phai model dealer thuc te (dealer co the da hedge mot phan, co vi the tu
  cac nguon khac ngoai san). Dung de dinh huong xac suat, KHONG dung nhu tin
  hieu tuyet doi.
- Voi 0DTE, T-to-expiry giam dan trong ngay -> Gamma tang manh ve cuoi
  phien -> cac muc Gamma Wall/Call Resistance/Put Support co the "sac net"
  hon va bien dong quanh cac muc do co the manh hon vao gio dong cua.
- Nen doi chieu voi range69 (range/breakout stats du an hien tai) de xac
  nhan them: vung Call Resistance/Put Support trung voi cac muc high/low
  session lich su cang tang do tin cay.
